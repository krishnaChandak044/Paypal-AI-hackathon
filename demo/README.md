# ReliefRail Demo Video

- `ReliefRail-3-minute-demo.mp4` is the finished 1920×1080, 30 fps demo pitch.
- Runtime: 2:56.8, kept under the hackathon's three-minute review limit.
- `captions.srt` contains timed English captions for YouTube.
- `voiceover.md` contains the complete narration.
- `make_demo.py` and `make_captions.py` are the local render sources.
- `assets/scene_*.png` are the editable storyboard frames.

The narration uses macOS Samantha speech synthesis at a measured rate. The ambient music bed is generated locally for this video. The product captures use the repository's synthetic screenshots, cropped to omit old toolbar labels; the remaining illustrations are explanatory graphics. No real payment is initiated by the video.

For Devpost, upload the MP4 to YouTube as public or unlisted with no password, add `captions.srt`, and paste the YouTube URL into the project submission. Review the video once before uploading, especially names and sample labels in screenshots.
